# tacplus-dev roadmap

The roadmap and the decision records of tacplus-dev, the platform: tacplus, kernel, the services, fleets and
loopers. Authoritative from 2026-10-27 (Jody: "move the tacplus-dev roadmap to tacplus-dev"). tacplus-dev must
work by itself, without SeaBeam.

- `decisions/`: dated decision records and huddle notes, `<topic>-<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`. Carried over with their
  history from `gitlab.com/seabeam/schematic/umbrella`, folder `roadmap/`, at `814ffde2`.
- `items/`: roadmap items (status, owner, what they're held on). Copied from `gitlab.com/seabeam/roadmap`,
  folder `items/`, at `7486574`: the platform items only. SeaBeam's product items (atelier, tenant
  relationships) stay in SeaBeam.

New records and items go here, never back into SeaBeam. Reference them from issues as
`tacplus-dev/roadmap:decisions/<file>`.
