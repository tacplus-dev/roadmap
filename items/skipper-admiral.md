# Skipper and Admiral move from SeaBeam to tacplus

- **Status:** next, high priority (Jody, 2026-11-01): "I think we move them up in priority now. This does not really delay
  Seabeam because we are simply moving stuff over from Seabeam to tacplus."
- **Decision:** `decisions/skipper-and-admiral-2026-11-01.md`:
  - a Skipper per tenant, spanning clusters;
  - an Admiral per deployment, in `/system`;
  - tenants talk to their Skipper, never the Admiral;
  - the Skipper dials out and holds a bidi channel to its Admiral.
- **Depends on:**
  - tacplus-dev/tacplus#259 (make-before-break stream rotation, now high), for the long-lived channel;
  - tacplus-dev/tacplus#265 (trace roles), for the Skipper and Admiral as the tenant and platform trace roles.

## Steps

1. **Survey what SeaBeam has.** The Skipper and Admiral as built in SeaBeam (gitlab.com/seabeam/sonar/backend and related):
   responsibilities, APIs, data, what's product persona and what's platform role. Record it here, read-only. SeaBeam isn't
   changed. Port commits carry `Ported-from:` the other way only by decision; tacplus-dev never depends on SeaBeam.
2. **Huddle with Jody:**
   - what each one does (responsibilities, the typed Skipper↔Admiral API, the tenant↔Skipper API);
   - what stays persona (SeaBeam) and what is platform (tacplus).
3. **Repositories:** `tacplus-dev/skipper` (tenant-owned, multi-cluster) and `tacplus-dev/admiral` (`/system`), both Rust and
   generated by tacplus. Created after the huddle, with Jody's OK.
4. **Build:**
   - the bidi channel on #259;
   - the audit of every Admiral message;
   - the Skipper's own policy for acting on requests;
   - deployed with every fleet by the operator, like the chaos agent.
