Suggestion: The gateway's tenant hops hold the tenant: the routed tid's SPIFFE ID verified at the handshake, and the blob byte paths dialled to that tenant's blobstore, not to any healthy pod
Type: Security
Summary: Since gateway#3 (2026-11-22) the gateway dials <svc>-<tid> for a call naming a tenant, but Resolver::tls_verifier (src/resolver.rs:279) narrows the backend's certificate to the bare service label (expect_service), so any tenant's instance of that service passes the handshake and only the instance's own backstop (check_routed_tenant) refuses the call after its bytes have crossed (gateway PROPOSALS 2026-11-15: 'a feature, needs design'; no issue). And the two hand-proxied byte paths, PUT /upload/<tid>/<token> and GET /b/<tid>/<token>, are not routed at all: BlobProxy::pick_backend (src/blob_proxy.rs:203) round-robins every healthy pod the health plane knows as 'blobstore' ('EITHER POD WILL DO FOR EITHER PATH', written for one blobstore a cluster) over a plaintext hop, while blobstore has been tenant-owned since blobstore#9 (2026-11-01: 'the gateway needs a rule for them ... Needs an issue on tacplus-dev/gateway, at Jody's request'; none exists). Verify spiffe://<td>/tenants/<tid>/services/<svc> at the handshake (a generated SpiffeServerCertVerifier that expects the namespace too, in tacplus) and route the byte paths by the tid in the path, refusing a segment that is not a tid, with an e2e_tenant_routing leg for each
Why: Tenant isolation at the edge: a tenant's upload or a call's bytes must never reach another tenant's instance, and today either does, on a wrong endpoint in the health index (the TLS hop) or on every other request (the byte paths: with N tenants, N-1 of N uploads and downloads land on another tenant's blobstore and fail 404, or worse are served). seatrials' first deploy is in flight (fleet-seatrials#1 and #4 closed 2026-11-23, #65 open) and bar B is sign-in on dev; a byte path that fails on every second request would be its first bug. Both gaps have sat in PROPOSALS, one for three weeks, because a looper may file a feature only on Jody's word. Also Correctness
